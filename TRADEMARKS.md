## Uses that are allowed without asking

* Referring to the project by name in articles, reviews, comparisons and documentation.
* Saying that your product or service **works with**, **is built on**, or **is compatible with**

* Redistributing **unmodified** official builds under the Wonslate name, with a link to this project
  and a clear statement that the build is unmodified.
* Using the name in the package metadata of a fork **only** to state its origin
  ("fork of Wonslate"), as long as the fork is clearly identified as such.
